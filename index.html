How it works

Take your pick from the agricultural supply chain and participate in vetted agribusiness projects backed by real land, multi-generational farming expertise, and crop insurance.

  • 01
  • 02

Phase 1

Select your farmshare & complete reservation.

Browse audited soil test records, farmer track records, crop varieties, and legal prospectuses. Allocate capital starting from as low as $500 per share.

Phase 2

Insured escrow & contract execution.

Your investment funds are secured in regulated, custodial escrow accounts. Capital is deployed only when planting benchmarks, water permits, and crop reinsurance are verified.

Phase 3

Track growth with satellite & drone imagery.

Receive quarterly NDVI satellite biomass scans, drone surveys, and regular audio/video dispatches from your assigned farm managers straight to your investor portal.

Phase 4

Harvest distribution & profit payout.

Upon harvest completion and wholesale delivery to certified food distributors, revenue is calculated, and your net yield is deposited directly into your linked bank account.
